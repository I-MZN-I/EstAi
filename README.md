# EstAi 🏠🤖

**EstAi** is a next-generation, AI-enhanced real estate platform designed to bridge the gap between property seekers and their dream homes. By blending data-driven insights with a seamless user experience, we make buying, selling, and renting smarter than ever before.

---

### 🚀 Core Features
* **Map Integration:** Visualize your next neighborhood with intuitive, interactive map layers.
* **AI Price Estimation:** Take the guesswork out of the market with intelligent valuation tools.
* **Property Listing:** Effortlessly list properties for sale or rent with a clean, high-conversion interface.
* **Curated Showcases:** Stay ahead of the curve with a dedicated section for trending properties and upcoming projects.

---

### 💡 Why EstAi?
Traditional real estate can feel overwhelming. **EstAi** leverages machine learning to provide transparency and accuracy, ensuring that every user—whether a first-time renter or a seasoned investor—has the tools they need to make confident decisions.
